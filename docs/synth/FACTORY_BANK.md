# Machinista Factory

264 original patches organized into 13 category banks plus Init: 250 retained previous sounds and 14 newly designed wavetable patches. No hardware-emulation claim.

The nine generated waveforms contain only mathematically synthesized harmonics. There are no third-party recordings or manufacturer ROM waveforms. Tine, reed and plucked timbres are synthesized approximations. Natural acoustic piano/guitar realism requires appropriate transient and multisample material.

The aggregate Factory JSON is retained for compatibility and packaging checks.
It is hidden from BANK and Sounds when all its patches are covered by the
category banks. Existing Factory selections resolve to their categories without
changing saved sound parameters. No patches are removed.

## Consolidation audit

Restored the full previous collection except two especially close flute variants and the explicitly excluded drum/percussion patches. Matching oscillator families alone is not a reason to remove a sound. All other strings, brass, keys, organs, basses and effects remain available. Melodic mallets, bells and plucks remain; kicks, snares, hats, claps, toms and noise percussion are excluded. The similarity scores are heuristic candidates, not proof of identical sounds.

- Oberista Flute Air → Junista Wind Flute: Very close spectral/envelope flute variant; no separate instrument category lost.
- Prophista Soft Flute → Junista Wind Flute: Close soft triangle/breath flute variant; other reed/woodwind/flute sounds retained.

## Installation and use

On the controller: select Machinista Synth, BANK Machinista Factory, then PATCH. WebUI Synth offers the same presets. Both paths load waveform assignments together with the parameters.

When installing elsewhere, upload the nine WAV files through WebUI Oscillators using cycle size 1024, then import the desired bank JSON files for Machinista Synth. The waveform content IDs are deterministic. Missing waveform assets produce an explicit error instead of playing an unrelated previously loaded waveform.

The former eight family banks and the original small factory bank are retired. Existing saved sound parameters remain in projects; their old preset-bank references may show MISSING BANK.
